# Corpus manifest

Confirm Milestone 1 authorization before discovery or acquisition. Prefer official Defensoría sources. Record report number/reference month without relying on filenames alone; official URL, retrieval time, original filename, bytes, SHA-256, pages, and status. Preserve alternate official byte versions and model their relationships. Never overwrite raw files, infer a missing report exists, or continue into parsing.
