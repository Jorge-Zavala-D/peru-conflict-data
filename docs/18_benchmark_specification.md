# Ten-report modern benchmark

Reports 260-269 are the first consecutive benchmark. Milestone 2 must create independently human-verified gold data; parser output is never gold truth. Coverage includes report metadata and identity evidence, sections, case blocks/codes/names/types/status/phases/geography/actors/demands (including separate theme/category/competent-entity columns), facts, mediation processes and dated dialogue, protests/date precision, violence/casualties/type, Defensoría action hierarchy, agreements/follow-up, alerts/type/risk/location, provenance, source-reported versus derived monthly indicators, and cross-month linkage.

Targets are at least 99% exact accuracy for central deterministic fields, at least 99% precision and recall for case detection, at least 99% source-page attribution, zero unresolved critical parser errors, all arithmetic discrepancies classified, and all medium-confidence links reviewed. These are targets, not guarantees. Any revision requires evidence, documentation, and Jorge's approval.
