# Limited evidence basis for AI/PI reconstruction

These seven owner-specified sources were checked on **6 October 2026** against
publisher, ACL Anthology, arXiv and official documentation records. They support
the adopted workflow's design; none measures this project's pipeline or certifies
its inherited accuracy targets. No broader literature review was undertaken.

| Verified source | Supported use and limit |
| --- | --- |
| Gilardi, Fabrizio, Meysam Alizadeh and Maël Kubli (2023), *ChatGPT outperforms crowd workers for text-annotation tasks*, PNAS, DOI [10.1073/pnas.2305016120](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2305016120). [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15056); [published article record](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10372638/). | Demonstrates useful automated classification against task-specific annotation standards. It does not establish complete relational extraction, source-object discovery or dispensability of expert source review. |

(2025), *PAPEA: A modular pipeline for the automation of protest event analysis*, Political Science Research and Methods, [publisher article](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/political-science-research-and-methods/article/papea-a-modular-pipeline-for-the-automation-of-protest-event-analysis/90205C53AAAAA675F21AC19178418E68), DOI [10.1017/psrm.2025.10013](https://doi.org/10.1017/psrm.2025.10013). The entries match the owner-specified identities. The ACL records confirm the
Gu paper's **2026** venue and full title, and the publisher records confirm the two
PSRM online publication dates above. The Gilardi published-article metadata and
abstract were available through the indexed published copy and the author preprint;
direct PNAS retrieval failed and a direct PMC page request met a browser check.
That access limitation is retained rather than describing an unavailable full-text
publisher read as completed. No numerical result is transferred to Peru's reports.
The Angelopoulos paper's identity and methods were checked through arXiv; direct
retrieval of the owner-supplied Science journal DOI returned HTTP 403, so no
successful Science full-text read is claimed.

The resulting methods commitments are source-based denominators, separate initial
machine and corrected outputs, exact source-first versus assisted review coverage,
discovery audits including apparent negatives, uncertainty appropriate to clustered
source units, and a PI decision before scale or release. The real pilot remains the
source of evidence about this project.
